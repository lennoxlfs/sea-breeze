Your Questions,

Six answers we wish every guest knew before they arrived. If your question isn't here, our team answers every email within the day.

01 Is Sea Breeze Beach House truly all-inclusive?

Yes. Your stay includes all meals and beverages (including premium spirits), nightly entertainment, non-motorised water sports, airport transfers, and access to six restaurants and five bars — with no signing of chits or surprise charges.

02 How far is Sea Breeze from Grantley Adams International Airport?

Approximately 15 minutes by car. Complimentary airport transfers are included with your all-inclusive stay.

03 Does every room have an ocean view?

Every room at Sea Breeze Beach House faces the Caribbean Sea. Classic Collection rooms offer pool, garden, or ocean views, and all Luxury Ocean Suites feature direct ocean-view balconies.

04 Are water sports included in the all-inclusive package?

Yes. Complimentary non-motorised water sports include snorkelling, kayaking, paddleboarding, and windsurfing on our Maxwell Coast beach.

05 Can I host a wedding at Sea Breeze Beach House?

Yes. Sea Breeze offers three bespoke wedding packages with certified wedding planners, a one-wedding-per-day guarantee, and beachfront or garden ceremony settings for up to 150 guests.

06 Is there a spa on-site?

Yes. Drift Spa offers massage therapies, bespoke facials, manicures, pedicures, and couples treatments using premium VIVANT® Skin Care products in a tranquil beachside setting.
