Sea Breeze Beach House is a 4-star, 122-room all-inclusive beachfront resort on Maxwell Coast in Christ Church, Barbados — with six restaurants, five bars, three pools, Drift Spa, and complimentary water sports all included in your stay.

Nestled on the warm sands of Maxwell Coast, Sea Breeze Beach House is Barbados' finest all-inclusive escape — where azure waters, world-class dining, and the warmth of Bajan hospitality meet effortlessly.

Whether you're celebrating love, creating family memories, or simply surrendering to the rhythm of the Caribbean, Sea Breeze is your perfect shore.

Before You Book

Your Questions,
Answered.

Is Sea Breeze Beach House truly all-inclusive?

Yes. Your stay includes all meals and beverages (including premium spirits), nightly entertainment, non-motorized water sports, airport transfers, and access to six restaurants and five bars — with no signing of chits or surprise charges.

How far is Sea Breeze from Grantley Adams International Airport?

Approximately 15 minutes by car. Complimentary airport transfers are included with your all-inclusive stay.

Does every room have an ocean view?

Every room at Sea Breeze Beach House faces the Caribbean Sea. Classic Collection rooms offer pool, garden, or ocean views, and all Luxury Ocean Suites feature direct ocean-view balconies.

Are water sports included in the all-inclusive package?

Yes. Complimentary non-motorized water sports include snorkeling, kayaking, paddleboarding, and windsurfing on our Maxwell Coast beach.

Can I host a wedding at Sea Breeze Beach House?

Yes. Sea Breeze offers three bespoke wedding packages with certified wedding planners, a one-wedding-per-day guarantee, and beachfront or garden ceremony settings for up to 150 guests.

Is there a spa on-site?

Yes. Drift Spa offers massage therapies, bespoke facials, manicures, pedicures, and couples treatments using premium VIVANT® Skin Care products in a tranquil beachside setting.
