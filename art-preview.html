There are places you visit, and there are places that stay with you. Sea Breeze is the second kind.

Kept simple. Kept true. Kept ours.

For forty years this stretch of the south coast has been our home. Guests return not for what's new, but for what doesn't change — the pace of the mornings, the way the water greets the sand, the warmth of the people who've always been here. We've been careful not to over-polish what the island gave us.
